* Net Control Operations Philosophy

Running a live trivia net on amateur radio (especially digital voice modes like
DMR, D-STAR, or System Fusion) requires balancing three critical priorities:
1. *Audio Discipline:* Keeping transmissions crisp, waiting for repeater tails to drop, and preventing link timeouts.
2. *Fair Participant Engagement:* Ensuring all check-ins receive equal airtime and answering opportunities regardless of callsign prefix or check-in order.
3. *Engaging Presentation:* Prompting trivia questions clearly, giving operators sufficient time to key up, and sharing interesting Net Control facts to foster discussion.

* TriviaNetDMR Scoring System

The scoring model is designed to reward participation while honoring trivia knowledge:
- *Check-in Point (1 pt):* Automatically awarded when an operator is checked into the roster. Encourages radio check-ins even if the operator is hesitant to answer questions.
- *Trivia Answer Point (1 pt):* Awarded (=y=) when the operator provides a correct or close answer to the question. Deductible (=x=) if awarded in error.
- *Bonus Point (1 pt):* Awarded (=b=) at Net Control's discretion for humorous, exceptionally clever, or insightful additional commentary. Deductible (=v=).
- *Total Score:* Automatically calculated as =Points(Checkin) + Points(Trivia) + Points(Bonus)=.

* Step-by-Step NCS Runbook

** Phase 1: Pre-Net Setup (15 Minutes Before Net)
1. *Select & Validate Question Deck:*
   #+BEGIN_SRC bash
     # Run pre-flight check to ensure the question file is error-free
     ./TriviaNetDMR --check Topic/Questions-1.md
   #+END_SRC
2. *Configure QRZ Credentials (Optional):*
   If live callbook lookup is desired:
   #+BEGIN_SRC bash
     export QRZ_USERNAME="KC8WXM"
     export QRZ_PASSWORD="my_qrz_password"
   #+END_SRC
   Otherwise, let the app run in *Offline Mock Mode*.
3. *Launch TriviaNetDMR:*
   #+BEGIN_SRC bash
     ./TriviaNetDMR
   #+END_SRC
4. *Verify Topic & Screen:*
   - Check the top header to confirm the active release version (e.g. =v0.1.2=) and QRZ connection mode.
   - On first run or new session, verify the roster is clean at Round 1 (or press =Backspace= / =k= to reset session).
   - If you wish to change topics on the fly, press =t= to open the Deck Picker, select the desired theme, and press =Enter=.
   - If you need a refresher on keyboard shortcuts, press =?= to view the Help Menu.

** Phase 2: Opening the Net & Calling for Check-ins
1. Key the radio and read the *Net Opening Script* (see template below).
2. Announce the trivia theme for the session.
3. Call for check-ins in groups (e.g. by call district, mobile stations, or open call).
4. For each checking-in station:
   - Press =c= to open the check-in dialog.
   - Enter the callsign and press =Enter=.
   - The operator appears immediately on screen and background QRZ lookup begins.
   - Acknowledge the operator by name and city once QRZ data populates.

** Phase 3: Conducting Trivia Rounds
1. *Prompting the Question:*
   - Ensure answer is hidden (=a= toggles visibility if you want to keep it private until revealed).
   - Read the question clearly over the air. Repeat once for mobile stations.
2. *Cycling the Queue:*
   - The participant table indicates the active operator with the =▶= green cursor.
   - Call on the active operator: /"Next up is Bradley, KE8WNV. Bradley, your answer for Round 1?"/
   - After the operator responds:
     - Press =y= if correct.
     - Press =b= if they offered an entertaining bonus fact.
   - Press =n=, =Enter=, or =↓= to advance to the next operator.
3. *Ending the Round & Rotating:*
   - When all participants in the queue have taken a turn, the status pane announces:
     #+BEGIN_QUOTE
     🏆 ROUND COMPLETED! 🏆 Press [r] for next round.
     #+END_QUOTE
   - Press =r= to rotate:
     - Increments the round number.
     - Shifts queue position (operator #2 becomes the new first turn).
     - Advances to the next trivia question in the deck automatically.

** Phase 4: Handling Late Check-ins & Corrections
- *Late Check-ins:* Simply press =c= and enter their callsign. They are automatically added to the roster and placed at the end of the current rotation queue.
- *Mistyped Callsigns:* Highlight the operator in turn, press =e=, enter the corrected callsign, and press =Enter=. Points are preserved and QRZ is re-queried.
- *Dropouts / Early Sign-offs:* Press =d= to confirm deletion from the active queue.

** Phase 5: Final Scores, Contest Export & Net Close
1. Once all trivia rounds are complete, press =f= to open the *Interactive Final Scoreboard*.
2. Announce the podium winners over the air:
   - 🥇 First Place
   - 🥈 Second Place
   - 🥉 Third Place
3. Press =s= to export contest standings:
   - Select CSV (=contest_results.csv=) for spreadsheet archiving.
   - Select JSON (=contest_results.json=) for web logging or DMR club archives.
4. Read the *Net Closing Script*, return the repeater/talkgroup to regular amateur use, and press =q= to exit.

* Standard Net Control Script Templates

** On-Air Net Opening Script
#+BEGIN_QUOTE
"Calling all amateur radio operators. This is [CALLSIGN], [NAME], acting as Net
Control Station for this evening's Amateur Radio Trivia Net.

This net meets weekly to promote camaraderie, technical knowledge, and good
operating practices across our DMR network. All licensed amateurs are warmly
welcomed to participate.

Tonight's trivia topic is: [TOPIC TITLE].

Scoring is simple: You will receive one point automatically just for checking
into the log, one point for each correct trivia answer, and optional bonus
points for interesting commentary.

We will now begin taking check-ins. Please transmit your callsign phonetically,
your name, and your location. Stations wishing to check in, please call now."
#+END_QUOTE
