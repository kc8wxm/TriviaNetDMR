### **10 Alternate Trivia Questions & Answers: Talk Like a Pirate Day**

**1\. Question:** In traditional nautical terminology, what was the beverage called **"grog"** originally made of?

* **Answer:** **Rum diluted with water** (often with lemon or lime juice added to prevent scurvy).  
* *Net Control Fact:* It was introduced in 1740 by British Vice Admiral Edward Vernon, nicknamed "Old Grog" because of the grogram coat he wore.

**2\. Question:** What gruesome naval punishment involved tying a rope to a crewman and dragging him underwater from one side of the vessel, underneath the hull, to the other?

* **Answer:** **Keelhauling.**  
* *Net Control Fact:* If the sailor didn't drown, the razor-sharp barnacles encrusting the ship's bottom usually inflicted severe wounds.

**3\. Question:** Which famous Welsh privateer and pirate sacked Panama City in 1671 and was later knighted by King Charles II, eventually becoming the Lieutenant Governor of Jamaica?

* **Answer:** **Sir Henry Morgan** (Captain Morgan).

**4\. Question:** In pirate folklore and maritime legend, what is the idiom used to describe the bottom of the sea as the final resting place of dead sailors?

* **Answer:** **Davy Jones' Locker.**

**5\. Question:** What is the maritime term for a sailor or crew officer responsible for the ship's rigging, anchors, sails, and deck operations, famously summoned via a shrill silver pipe or whistle?

* **Answer:** **Boatswain** (pronounced *Bosun*).

**6\. Question:** Which notorious female pirate sailed alongside "Calico Jack" Rackham and Anne Bonny during the Golden Age of Piracy, famously disguised in men's clothing?

* **Answer:** **Mary Read.**  
* *Net Control Fact:* Both Mary Read and Anne Bonny escaped immediate execution after capture by "pleading their bellies" (declaring they were pregnant).

**7\. Question:** What is the common nautical term for the raised deck at the very rear (aft) of a sailing ship, typically where the captain and officers maintained watch?

* **Answer:** **The Poop Deck.**  
* *Net Control Fact:* The term derives from the French word *la poupe*, meaning the stern.

**8\. Question:** In real 18th-century pirate crews, how was the captain chosen and held accountable?

* **Answer:** **By a democratic vote of the crew.**  
* *Net Control Fact:* Unlike traditional naval vessels with strict hierarchies, pirate ships operated under written "Articles" and could vote their captain out if they were unhappy with his leadership.

**9\. Question:** Operating off the coast of China in the early 19th century, history's most successful and powerful pirate leader commanded the Red Flag Fleet of several hundred ships. What was her name?

* **Answer:** **Ching Shih** (or Zheng Yi Sao / Madame Zheng).  
* *Net Control Fact:* She commanded upwards of 70,000 to 80,000 pirates and outfought the Qing dynasty, the Portuguese, and the British before negotiating an honorable retirement and keeping her loot.

**10\. Question:** What is the nautical phrase shouted by a lookout upon first sighting land from the crow's nest or masthead?

* **Answer:** **"Land ho\!"**
